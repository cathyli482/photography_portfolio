About

Behind a camera can only be one pair of eyes, and a shutter only has one finger to press—it’s a second that belongs only to me. The thrill of making quick decisions in fleeting moments rushes with satisfaction and joy. The meaning of film is that a shutter pressed a month ago leaves me forgetting what was in the frame, where I was, or who was beside me. But when looking back, all the memories become clear again. It is as if opening a gift of time, delayed.

I am currently an undergraduate student at the University of Pittsburgh, where I always chase to capture the mundane moments of my life. I’m actively seeking opportunities to grow my craft, to where I can push past what I know into what I haven’t tried yet.

— Cathy
